**This is a layout and envelope model, not shop drawings.** Every part is a box showing position, outer size and clearances. The fabricator should produce their own sheet-metal drawings (bends, flanges, hinges, weld details) from it and send them back for approval before cutting.

## Envelope

- Overall **1050 W × 820 D × 1900 H**. Fits a standard 900 × 2040 door when moved on a trolley sideways. Estimated empty weight 190–220 kg; floor-anchor holes in the plinth.
- Leave 100 mm behind and 150 mm above for condenser airflow.

## Materials and finish

- Body: 1.2 mm CRCA inner and outer skins, 40 mm PUF (40 kg/m³) between; door skin and fascia 2 mm CRCA. Powder coat 60–80 µm, RAL 7016 body with RAL 6033 accent, after 7-tank pretreatment.
- Food-contact and pickup areas: SS 304.
- Glass: 6 mm toughened + 12 mm gap + 6 mm toughened, with anti-fog heater film or silica-gel spacer.
- Security: 3-point door latch driven by the 24 V solenoid plus a mechanical key override, concealed hinges, anti-pry flange around the door, floor anchoring.

## Restocking features to keep

1. Door opens 110° to the left; trays pull out fully on telescopic slides and tilt down 15° for top-loading.
2. Each tray connects with one 14-way plug so it can be lifted out.
3. Slot labels (A1…F8) on the tray front lip, matching the kiosk screen and admin portal.
4. Door opens only by service PIN on the screen or by the admin portal; every opening is logged and an unlogged opening raises an alarm.
